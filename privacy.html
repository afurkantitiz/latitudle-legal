Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026
Applies to Latitudle for Android and iPhone.

Summary

Latitudle collects nothing about you. There are no accounts and no analytics, and the app has no server of its own. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

The only party that can receive data is Google, through the advertising SDK behind the optional rewarded ads (see Ads). The game is fully playable offline.

What stays on your device

The app keeps one settings file in its private storage: your guesses and finished puzzles, your statistics, your passport (countries found and guessed), your preferences (language, distance unit, daily reminder) and whether you own Latitudle+. The app never sends any of it anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The only ads are optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob: a short ad in exchange for a hint, a streak rescue, extra blitz seconds, or three more rounds after the three free daily rounds of a free-play mode. There are no banners, no ads between screens and no ad at start-up. An ad plays only after you tap an offer and confirm. The daily puzzle is never limited, and offline or with Latitudle+ there is no limit and no ads.

When the app starts with a connection, it asks Google's consent service (UMP) whether consent is needed and shows Google's consent form where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland); Settings → Ads → Ad preferences lets you change your answer at any time. Once consent allows ads, the app starts the Google Mobile Ads SDK for that session, whether or not you watch an ad. Google may then receive data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with an ad, to serve, measure and secure ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences (G). See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Latitudle itself never sees or stores this data. On iPhone, the app does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and requests only non-personalised ads, so Google does not receive your IDFA. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchase (Latitudle+)

Latitudle+ is an optional one-time purchase that removes the ad offers and the daily round limit and adds extra globe themes. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; Latitudle never sees your payment details. The app only stores whether you own it and asks the store to confirm it from time to time.

Permissions

On Android:

  • Notifications — only if you turn on the optional daily reminder, a local alarm on your device.
  • Run at startup — so the reminder survives a reboot; used for nothing else.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID — for the consent service and the optional rewarded ads.
  • Billing — only for the optional Latitudle+ purchase.

On iPhone the app asks for one permission only: notifications, if you turn on the daily reminder. The game works the same if you decline.

Backups

Your device's own backup (Android Auto Backup, iCloud or a computer backup) may include the app's settings file. That is a transfer between you and Google or Apple under their terms; Latitudle does not read or send it. You can turn these backups off in your device settings.

Children

Latitudle is not directed at children; its target audience is 16 and over. The app collects no personal data from anyone.
